# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Verity to be a place where anyone can contribute without being
harassed or demeaned, whatever their experience, background, identity or
location. Be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review. Critique the
  change, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Give credit where it is due.
- Say plainly what you tested and what you did not. Honest reporting of
  results matters here as much as in the code.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and disagree without hostility.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated off-topic noise.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including automated or low-effort pull requests opened to farm
  rewards, and misrepresenting work you did not do.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, chat) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour
breaks this code. Maintainers will explain the decision when they can do so
without exposing the person who reported it.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository:
<https://github.com/DevQwinB/Verity/security/advisories/new>
(mark the report "Conduct"), or contact a maintainer directly through their
GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter is protected
from retaliation.
